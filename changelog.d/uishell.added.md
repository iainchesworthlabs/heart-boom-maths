**The title, Settings, About and parental gate screens.** The title shows the best score, the coins, PLAY (or CONTINUE and NEW GAME with a line saying what the saved run was) and opens TOP 10 and Settings. Settings has the Sound effects and Music switches, which say ON or OFF in words as well as colour, a Game Center switch only where the device has a leaderboard, and the build line. About shows the version, revision, branch and date, the font licences, the licence and the third-party notices in a scrolling text, and the privacy policy and source code links. Every link and the Game Center switch ask a grown-up first: a number in words, typed on a keypad or a keyboard, with OPEN grey until it is right and a new number after a wrong one. The pause screen now opens Settings.
