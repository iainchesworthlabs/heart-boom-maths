**The game has haptics on phones.** A light tick on a button press, a crisp tap for a right answer, a buzz for a wrong one, a bump for a streak step, a warning when the alert starts, success for a cleared level, a short run of three beats for the win and a heavy thud for the bomb. They use UIKit feedback on iPhone, the system's touch feedback on Android (no permission) and vibration in a phone browser, are never the only cue, stop at once on a pause or when the app leaves the foreground, and have a Haptics switch in Settings that is on by default and shown only where the device can make them.
