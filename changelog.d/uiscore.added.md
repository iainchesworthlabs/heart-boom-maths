**The result and score screens.** Level clear shows the points breakdown, the coins earned and the wallet, then NEXT LEVEL and a line about the next level. The win screen shows the 1,000-coin bonus only when it was actually paid, with KEEP GOING and FINISH. Game over shows BOOM! only when the bomb went off, with the level reached, score, best score, coins earned and the wallet, and PLAY AGAIN, TOP 10 and HOME. A score that earns a place opens the name screen: the name takes capitals and spaces up to the length in the tuning, from a hardware keyboard or an on-screen keyboard on touch devices, and SAVE needs at least one letter. The Top 10 shows rank, name and score, marks the new entry with a NEW tag as well as colour, and has a message for an empty list.
