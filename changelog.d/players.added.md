**A family can share the game, with a player each.** The title shows who is playing, and a tap on it opens WHO'S PLAYING, where up to four players are picked, added, named and renamed, and removed with the parental gate (never the last one). Each player has their own coins, win bonus and best score, and a saved game is CONTINUE only for the player who paused it. A player with a name has a top 10 score saved under that name with no name screen, a player with no name is asked once, and NEW BEST! now means a better score than that player's own best. Players are switched from the title only.
