**The game builds for iPhone and iPad, with an optional Game Center score.** The `ios-game-release` preset links the game statically against Qt for iOS, with a property list, entitlements and a placeholder icon of its own, and a macOS workflow compiles it and checks the bundle. On Apple devices the Settings switch (off by default, behind the parental gate) turns on a Game Center service that installs its sign-in handler only then and sends one number, the score of a finished game, and nothing else about the player. The leaderboard identifier is a placeholder until the owner creates the leaderboard.
