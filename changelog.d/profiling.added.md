**The game can be profiled, in a release too.** Tracy is built into the Windows, Linux and macOS builds, dormant: nothing starts, listens or collects until the game is started with `--profile` or `HBM_PROFILE=1`, and then it listens on the loopback interface only, so a release can be profiled with no rebuild. The web, Android and iOS builds, and the sanitizer and coverage builds, have no Tracy. The rules library, the app layer, the save, the sound and the scene graph's frame have zones and plots, the GPU's frame time is plotted (with a GPU zone on Direct3D 11), and `--profile-run <seconds>` plays scripted games on a real window and writes the frame statistics of each screen. `windows-msvc-profile` and `linux-profile` build it for taking a trace. `docs/profiling.md` says what listens and when, how to capture a trace and what the first one found.
