**The back key works on every screen, and an interrupted game is handled in more cases.** On Android the back key now pauses a game, resumes from the pause screen, leaves the Top 10 and goes home from the game-over card, and does nothing on the level-clear, win and name screens, where it used to close the app; it also works on About, which ignored it. A saved game that is not a paused level is no longer offered a screen with no numbers, the parental gate closes when the app leaves the foreground, QUIT during the held look of an answer no longer waits for the hold, and the Top 10 button says NEW GAME while a saved game waits. A new page, User journeys, maps every screen and what is saved at each step.
