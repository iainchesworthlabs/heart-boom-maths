**A family's devices can share players and the top 10 through iCloud.** On an iPhone or iPad a grown-up can turn on iCloud sync in Settings, under Game Center, behind the parental gate. The players (names, coins, win bonus, best scores) and the top 10 then stay the same on every device signed in to the same Apple Account, and coins earned on two devices are added together. It is off by default. The game has no account or server of its own: it uses the iCloud of the Apple Account already on the device, nothing reaches the developer, and with the switch off the game never contacts iCloud. Turning it off leaves the copy in iCloud as it is. The Settings switches, the saved game and who is playing stay on each device.
