**The play screen and the design system.** A QML design system (theme, comic button, card, badge, coin wallet, star burst, halftone page) and the heart bomb with its calm, alert and boom states. The play screen draws the question, four tiles, the fuse bar, points, multiplier and hint button at phone, tablet and desktop sizes, holds a right or wrong answer on screen for a moment with a shape or motion cue for every colour, and has a pause screen. An app shell shows the screen that matches the game state, so a game can be played from the title to the game-over card.
