**The game makes sound.** Each effect of the feedback table (chime, buzz, rising tone, ticking, pop, fanfare, whoosh) and two looping music beds play through Qt Multimedia, honour the two Settings switches, stop when the app leaves the foreground and start on the first tap in a browser. The sound files are placeholders synthesised by a script, to be replaced by a sound designer. A build without Qt Multimedia, a test run and a machine with no sound device stay silent.
